Arizona's live music in one place — arena tours, club nights, jazz rooms, metal shows and underground raves. Compiled from venue calendars, ticketing pages and community trackers like 19hz.info and Jazz PHX. Filter by genre, city and room size.

Verified = a confirmed listing. Underground = venue is TBA or released by the promoter the day of. The gray badge is room size: Large (Arena), Medium (Club/Theater) or Underground (Warehouse/DIY). Genre tags can carry a sub-genre (like Rock | Pop Punk) for flavor — it isn't a filter. Each listing names where it was found, since no single source catches everything. Listings are compiled from public sources and can change, sell out or be canceled — confirm with the venue or ticket page before you go. Last updated Oct 19, 2026.
